# MoveWell Design System

## Product
MoveWell is a home exercise companion that a physiotherapist sets up for an older patient (65 to 85). The patient stands 2 to 3 metres from a laptop webcam; the app counts repetitions and speaks short encouragement in English or Hindi. Video never leaves the device. Every screen has exactly one primary action. It must feel like a calm, trustworthy, market-ready health product, not a startup landing page, fitness app or dashboard. Design for laptop 1440x900 first.

## Colour
- Background oat #F3EEE4; raised surface #FBF8F2; hairline rules #DDD5C7
- Text #1B2B23; secondary text #4A5A51 (never lighter)
- Primary forest green #1F4D3A; text on primary #FFFFFF
- Deep forest #163A2C for the live session, rest and next-up screens, with oat #F3EEE4 text
- Terracotta #B5533C only for celebration (the Done screen panel and the wordmark dot); never for buttons
- Warning amber #8F4300 on #FFF1E2
- All text meets WCAG AAA (7:1); display numbers at least 4.5:1

## Typography
- Display: Bricolage Grotesque, weights 600 to 800, letter-spacing -2%. Use for page titles, the wordmark and big numbers.
- Text: Figtree, weights 400, 500, 600. Use for everything else.
- Accent: handwriting in EMS Allure, a single-line script, drawn by a pencil at a steady speed. Used only at signature moments: the "Well" in the wordmark, the person's name in the greeting, "Well done" on the finish screen, and the live rep count and rest countdown. Falls back to Instrument Serif italic for characters the script lacks, such as Devanagari.
- Scale: page title 64px Bricolage 700; section heading 32px Bricolage 600; body 22px Figtree; secondary 18px Figtree; labels 24px Figtree 600; buttons 22px Figtree 600; rep counter 200px, handwritten.
- Minimum text size anywhere: 18px. No italics outside the accent moments above.

## Shape and layout
- Buttons: rectangles, 64px tall, 14px corner radius, wide or full column width. Primary filled forest green; secondary outlined 2px forest green. No pill shapes anywhere.
- Segmented controls: 64px tall, rectangular segments, 14px outer radius, selected segment forest green with white text.
- Inputs: 64px tall, 22px text, 14px radius, 2px #DDD5C7 border, forest green border on focus.
- Separate content with whitespace and hairline rules, never with boxed cards. At most one raised surface per screen.
- Colour blocking instead of cards: full-width forest green bands, full-height panels.
- Photos: 20px radius, large, may bleed to the edge of their section.
- Focus ring: 3px #B5533C outline, 3px offset.
- Touch and click targets at least 56px.

## Imagery
Real photography of older South Asian adults (and some other backgrounds) doing gentle exercises in bright, ordinary homes with a sturdy chair. Natural light, calm and dignified, not stock-smiley. Icons: simple 2px outline, only where they add meaning.

## Navigation
- Top bar: wordmark "MoveWell" (Bricolage Grotesque 800, forest green, small terracotta dot) on the left. Text links "Today", "Exercises", "Your week" and a text button "Settings" on the right. No icon-only buttons, no avatar.
- First-run setup screens show only the wordmark and the step, no navigation links.
- Live session, rest and next-up screens are full screen with no top bar.
- No footer.

## Voice
Plain, warm, second person, short sentences, like a kind physiotherapist: "Good morning, Asha." "Today's session: 8 minutes." "Lift both arms slowly." "Nice. That's 6." Say "times" or "movements", never "reps", "angle" or "threshold". No taglines, no hype.

## Never use
- Small uppercase letter-spaced labels above sections (eyebrow labels)
- Italic serif words mixed into headlines
- Gradient blobs, glassmorphism, glows
- Pill-shaped buttons, chips or badges
- Grids of identical white rounded cards with shadows
- Dashboard stat tiles
- Stick figures, clip art, emoji
- Status chips such as frame rates or "webcam active"
- More than one primary button per screen
